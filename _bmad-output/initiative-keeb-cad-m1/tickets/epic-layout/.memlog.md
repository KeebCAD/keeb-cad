- (requirement) R1 (parent R1; architecture AD-3): groups and slots are placed by one anchor + offset (translate, then rotate about pivot) to any slot attach point, group origin, vertex or construction point, by id; moving a node moves everything anchored to it, transitively; a command that would create a cycle is rejected, a loaded cycle reports anchor-cycle.
- (requirement) R2 (parent R2; architecture AD-4): children are arranged free or as a chain (+x or +y) whose step is a default anchor with gap; rotation carries along the chain, an explicit anchor stops the carry and keeps the index, empty slots still advance it; Corney Island's cumulative column stagger and 3-key thumb fan are authored without absolute coordinates and resolve within ±0.05 mm of the Ergogen fixture.
- (requirement) R3 (parent R3; architecture AD-4, AD-5): design pitch-x and pitch-y are independent of switch type, overridable per group, with u meaning pitch-x on x-tagged and pitch-y on y-tagged properties and unit-mismatch on untagged ones; slots are 1u or 1.5u; groups reference a key cell, inherited from the nearest ancestor and overridable per group; Choc v2 at 19 mm pitch is expressible; pitch below the switch's minimum reports pitch-below-min and is never adjusted.
- (requirement) R4 (parent R4; architecture AD-6): a split-reversible design authors the left side; model.resolve derives the right side by reflection with side-qualified ids and swapped attach points; population sets fitted per side per slot and an unfitted slot keeps its place and index; geometric right-side overrides are rejected; split-mirrored and unibody files load and report unsupported-form.
- (requirement) R5 (parent R8; architecture AD-5): the catalogue schema (components, pins and roles, minimum pitch, 3D envelope, cutouts with role and target plate/pcb/case, keep-outs, fixed tracks, part parameters with defaults) exists in model; library holds versioned Choc v2 hotswap and M2 screw entries with their cutouts; a slot holds a catalogue part pinned by id and version and anchors to any node.
- (requirement) R6 (parent R17; architecture Consistency Conventions, Violations): the layout stage resolves placements live and reports overlap, anchor-cycle, pitch-below-min, unsupported-form, unit-mismatch and unresolvable-reference as {kind, severity, ids, message} with side-qualified ids; a design problem never throws.
- (requirement) R7 (EXPERIENCE.md Interaction Primitives: chain drag rule, multi-selection, write-back, re-anchor): model commands move nodes by a world delta converted into each node's anchor frame (only selected nodes with no selected ancestor move); in a chain the along-direction part goes to gap and the perpendicular part to offset.y, and a compensate option takes the same change off the next step; rotate changes the deg term; write-back changes a plain literal, or the last literal mm/deg term of an expression, appending one if none, rounded to 0.01 mm and 0.1 deg; re-anchor keeps the node's world placement and flattens its offsets to plain numbers relative to the new anchor; each is one undo step.
