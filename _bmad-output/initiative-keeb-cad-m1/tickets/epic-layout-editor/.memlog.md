- (decision) 2026-10-26 the 2D canvas rendering technology (SVG, Canvas 2D or WebGL) goes to bmad-architecture as a Stack entry, in the same run as the 3D library question (initiative item 3); every canvas story waits on it.
- (requirement) R1 (parent R1, R2; EXPERIENCE.md 2D pointer map, Chain drag rule, Multi-selection): the Layout canvas adds groups (G) and slots (S), duplicates and deletes; left-drag on a key moves it and on a group frame's tab moves the group through epic-layout's move commands, Alt-drag (Ctrl-drag fallback) compensates; click, Shift-click and box select; wheel zoom to cursor, middle or Space drag and trackpad pan; F fits; the tree and canvas selection stay synced and the viewport is shared across 2D workspaces; one gesture is one undo step.
- (requirement) R2 (parent R1, R2; EXPERIENCE.md Rotation handle, Nudge, Rotate): the rotation handle rotates a key or group about its pivot with a delta readout, Shift snapping 5 deg; [ and ] rotate 1 deg (Shift 5); arrows nudge 0.25 / 1 / 0.05 mm, set in the nudge-step control, Alt+arrow compensates; a burst is one gesture; these act only while the canvas has focus.
- (requirement) R3 (parent R1; EXPERIENCE.md Anchor picker, Re-anchor, Pick modes): the anchor picker sets an anchor by clicking a target and an attach point (3x3 grid or canvas) or by typed id and Tab, with cycle-creating targets dimmed; re-anchoring keeps the node in place, flattens its offsets and says so in a toast; there is no drag-to-anchor.
- (requirement) R4 (parent R3; EXPERIENCE.md Inspector, Design settings, Expression field): the inspector shows the selection's anchor, offset, chain, population, part and key cell as expression fields, mixed values for multi-selection; with nothing selected, Design settings show the form read-only, pitch-x, pitch-y and the default key cell; groups show pitch and key-cell overrides with inherits; an invalid value such as u on rotation shows its error and keeps the last valid value.
- (requirement) R5 (parent R4; EXPERIENCE.md Both halves, Population toggle): both halves are drawn; the right half reads as derived (mirror axis, lower opacity until selected), is selectable, and its geometry fields are read-only with derived from left/<id>; Fitted: left / right toggles population per side, as one undo step for a multi-selection.
- (requirement) R6 (parent R8; EXPERIENCE.md Part field): P places a slot with the Part picker open; the Part field searches the catalogue by name and changing a part is one undo step.
- (requirement) R7 (parent R17; EXPERIENCE.md Violations panel, Canvas marks): layout violations show on the canvas (overlap solid or dashed, anchor-cycle path, glyph on ids) and in the violations panel (V), grouped by workspace and kind, errors first; a click selects the named ids, fits and flashes them; F8 and Shift+F8 step; a violation leaves only when fixed.
- (requirement) R8 (EXPERIENCE.md Shortcuts, Focus and regions, Esc precedence, Accessibility Floor; DESIGN.md): shortcuts bind physical keys and are off while a field has focus; Esc does the innermost step; canvas focus has a ring; reduced motion is honoured; canvas marks reach 3:1 contrast in both themes and handles keep the canvas hit radius at any zoom; Alt-drag and Ctrl chords are checked by hand in Chromium and Firefox.
- (constraint) Initiative C1 and C3 bind; desktop Chromium and Firefox at a 1366x768 floor (EXPERIENCE.md Foundation).
