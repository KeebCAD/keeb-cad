Group frame margin (shape C decided: rounded union of the keys)

Frames are drawn beneath the keys, so where a frame passes under another group's key its line is hidden there. Moments: pointer on index-bottom (the column nearest the fan), matrix selected, pointer on thumb-mid. Keys are 18 mm on a 19 mm pitch: keys in a column are 1 mm apart, so a union needs at least 0.5 mm to join them; columns are 1 mm apart; the fan's top is 1.6 mm below index's bottom. The last row keeps a fixed 6 px on screen and shows the same view at three zoom levels.
