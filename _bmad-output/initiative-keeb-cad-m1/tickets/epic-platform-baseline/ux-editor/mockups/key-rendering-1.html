Key rendering on the Layout canvas

Same scene in each: inner column (2 keys) and the 3-key thumb fan in its group frame; thumb-home selected (Mauve), its anchor link to thumb-tucky (Teal), overlap with thumb-tucky (Red). Canvas Surface0 with a Surface1 line grid.

(a) Filled keycaps

Solid keycap shapes (Surface2) with a slightly lighter top face. Reads as a physical keyboard.

(b) Outlined keys

Keycap outline only, with the switch cutout drawn inside. Drafting feel; geometry under keys stays visible.

(c) Tinted fill + outline

Translucent fill with a clear outline and the slot id inside. Between (a) and (b): solid enough to read, see-through enough for geometry.
