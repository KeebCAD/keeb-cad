Smoothing the fan’s pinched outline (shape C, margin = key gap = 1 mm, tab a)

At 1 mm the fan's outline joins only at narrow waists between its rotated keys (corners 0.98 and 1.34 mm apart), so it reads as three pieces. Each row is a general rule tried on the fan, the fan rotated 25°, matrix and a column. “Closing” grows the outline by r and shrinks it back by r: notches narrower than about 2r fill, the outside still hugs the keys.
