keeb-cad · colour themes, round 1

Five Catppuccin variations for the hybrid layout (5 workspace tabs, thin left rail, docked inspector, status strip). Each shows the Layout workspace at 0.6× of a 1366 × 696 viewport, its Latte counterpart beside it, a PCB-workspace crop at the same theme, the token chips for every role, and WCAG contrast for UI text on the inspector panel and the top/status surface. The variations differ on the two open questions: density (compact 12/22 · medium 13/26 · calm 14/30 px font/row) and canvas treatment (Crust + dots · Base + lines · Mantle blueprint · per-workspace tint · lifted Surface0). Hex values per role live in the <style> comments.

Contrast: every dark flavour passes AA for text, muted, warning and error on panel and surface. Latte fails for warning (Peach / Yellow ≈ 2.2–2.6:1) and is borderline for muted Subtext0 (3.7–4.4:1) and error on Crust/Mantle (4.1–4.5:1); a Latte fix would be muted → Subtext1 and a warning text token outside the palette (or warning shown as icon + Text-coloured label).
