- (decision) Drag in a chain (adopted from architecture memlog line 13 / paper-test-v0): dragging a key changes that key's own step (gap/offset from previous sibling); later siblings in the chain follow. - (decision) Drag on an expression-valued property: append a mm (or deg for rotation) correction term (e.g. 0.25u -> 0.25u + 0.37mm); later drags update that same trailing literal term rather than adding new terms. Parametric intent preserved; plain-literal properties just change their value.
- (decision) Canvas selection level: click on a key selects the key (slot). Each group is drawn as a grabbable frame; clicking its handle or empty space inside it selects the group. Dragging a group follows the same chain rule (changes its step; later sibling groups follow; Alt compensates).
- (decision) Nudge: arrow keys move selection; step configurable via a nudge-step setting in the status bar, defaults arrow 0.25mm, Shift+arrow 1mm, Ctrl+arrow 0.05mm.
