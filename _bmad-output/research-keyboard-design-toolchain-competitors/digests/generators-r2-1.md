### Synthesis for the decision

- **Moving one key updates the plate, case and footprint placement.** Ergogen solves this for people who write configs. Cosmos solves it for people building 3D-printed cases and handwired or flex-PCB boards.
- **Moving one key updates the trace routing.** Only partly solved in Ergogen: a router footprint stores per-key routes relative to the key's point. Routes between keys, and the tool that writes routes back into the config, are unverified, and the original tool has been unmaintained since 2023. ceoloide carries a `utility_router`, but its mechanism was not checked. Cosmos avoids routing entirely by using a generic flex PCB that the builder cuts to fit.
- **Moving one key updates the firmware layout.** Cosmos solves this, but only with its own Lemon controllers. No tool was found that does this from an Ergogen config.
- **Ergogen core is stagnant** (last push 2025-11). The active work in 2026 is in footprint libraries.

📐 Angle: Cosmos's approach of standard electronics with no routing may be a deliberate product choice, not a gap. People building PCB-less 3D-printed boards may never need routed PCBs. The demand for "routing that survives a key move" may sit mostly with Ergogen-style people who make flat custom PCBs.

## Leads worth chasing

- Read yanshay's `docs/ergogen_kicad_plugin.md` to confirm the round-trip from KiCad back into the config, and what it does with routes between keys.
- Read ceoloide's `utility_router.js` source and commit history: is it a port of yanshay's, and has it been updated for KiCad 8?
- Check ceoloide's other repos, such as an Ergogen template or starter repo and the Corney Island board, for full routed Ergogen configs.
- Look at the list of Ergogen forks pushed recently, and the branches beyond `develop`, for any v5 or KiCad 9 work. Also check the Ergogen Discord announcements channel (not reachable this run).
- Check the Cosmos blog under "Releases" for the 2026 roadmap: routed PCBs, more controllers, and what the rianadon/cosmos-keyboard-pcbs repo is doing.
- Look for other Ergogen firmware bridges, for example scripts that produce a ZMK physical layout or matrix transform from Ergogen points (try "ergogen zmk-physical-layout", "ergogen keymap-drawer").
- Find the Cosmos showcase count and the Discord invite member counts for both projects.
